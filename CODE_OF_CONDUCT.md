# Code of conduct

NorthCX is built by and for people who run customer support, and that work is about treating people well. We hold the project's spaces (issues, reviews, discussions, chat) to the same standard.

**Expected:** be respectful and assume good faith; give feedback on the work, specifically and kindly; welcome people at every level of experience; credit others' work; respect privacy, and never post customer data or secrets, even in examples.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory jokes or language; sharing others' private information; sustained disruption of discussions; deliberately misleading or spam contributions.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and ask someone to leave a space. To report a concern about conduct, use GitHub's private reporting for this repository (the same route as `SECURITY.md`) or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody is penalised for reporting in good faith.

This applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
